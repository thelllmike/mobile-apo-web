Bluetooth printers

58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers. The receipt prints by itself after payment.

WhatsApp receipts

No printer? Send the receipt as a picture or a PDF instead.

Questions

Before you download

Do I need a printer?

No. Every receipt can go out on WhatsApp as a picture or a PDF. If you do have one, Mobile POS works with 58 mm and 80 mm Bluetooth thermal printers.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. When the connection drops, bills are kept on the phone and sent up by themselves once you are back online. You need internet to sign up and to sign in the first time.

Is the whole app in Sinhala?

The billing screens, settings and receipts are in Sinhala and English, and you can switch language at any time.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Each cashier signs in with their own phone number and password, and you choose what they may do, such as giving discounts or voiding a bill. Prices, stock and reports stay behind the owner PIN.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones. Download it from Google Play.
